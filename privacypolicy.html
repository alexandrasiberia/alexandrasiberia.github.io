Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 03/25/2025

Thank you for visiting Kundalini Activation with Alexandra Siberia ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information:

  • For Individual Sessions: Information you send by email when booking a session.
  • For Online & Group Sessions: Payments for tickets are processed via Eventbrite.com, and we do not collect or store payment details.
  • General Usage Data: We may collect non-personal information such as browser type, pages visited, and interaction with our content for website improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely to:

  • Contact you regarding individual sessions.
  • Facilitate your registration for group and online sessions via Eventbrite.

We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal information to third parties. Your contact details are used exclusively for communication related to your sessions.

4. Email Communications

We do not send automatic promotional emails. Any communication from us will be directly related to your inquiries or session bookings.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to Eventbrite.com for session ticket purchases. Please review their privacy policy before providing your payment details.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. To do so, please contact us at Ikabyalexandra@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy
