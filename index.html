What is Kundalini Activation?

KA is an ancient healing modality, a powerful form of energy work with the potential to induce transcendental states of awareness and facilitate deep personal transformation.

It is an activation of your Life Force energy. It opens the path to profound awareness, harmony, and wholeness.

How Does It Work?

I hold individual and group sessions both online and offline, during which you immerse yourself in a state of deep relaxation.
The energy and consciousness of higher dimensions, transmitted during the session, begin to align and awaken your own Kundalini energy, activating your dormant potential and initiating your inner self-healing mechanism.

During the activation process, energy blockages are removed, and the life force within you is awakened.

No prior experienceis required.

All you need is an openness to embrace a new experience.

What happens during the session?

*

Before the session, it's important to complete some preparation. You can find it in the event description when you sign up.

1

During the Kundalini activation session, you lie on a mat, relax your body and mind, and let go of all expectations.

2

Your eyes are closed, your muscles are relaxed, and you remain open to any sensations, feelings, and energy manifestations.

3

The energy transmission lasts for an hour, accompanied by music throughout the session.

4

During a group session, I work simultaneously with the group as a whole and with each participant individually.

5

At times, I may touch energy centers or meridian points on your body. If you are uncomfortable with physical touch, I can work with you at a distance.

6

You will remain conscious and present throughout the session, although your mind may occasionally shift into a meditative state.

7

After the session, there is a sharing circle where everyone can exchange experiences, share insights, and ask questions.

The full session duration is 2 hours (including an introduction, one-hour energy transmission, and a sharing circle).

To discover more about Kundalini Activation energy transmissions, watch the video.

KA energy transmission is a safe way of working with Kundalini energy.

It is safe because KA is not a forceful method, which is different from breathwork or movement. KA is a path of surrender, not a path of will.

During the session, facilitator holds a specific frequency. Your Kundalini energy starts to attune to that frequency. No foreign energy is introduced into your system. This method is unique and differs from the common practices you may encounter online.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is KA a safe way to work with Kundalini energy?

KA is a non-forceful method of working with Kundalini energy. It’s a path of surrender. There is no excessive force applied to the individual, manipulating or accelerating the process. The facilitator and participants fully surrender to the ever-knowing energy of the Source. This energy knows exactly what you need. It’s beyond the limitations of the human mind. It’s unconditional love flowing through your body. The process might be intense for some, but it means you are ready for it. If the person is not ready, the process will pass unnoticed - they might even say, "I didn’t feel anything."

What are the benefits of Kundalini activation?

Release of old traumas and energy blocks. A sense of harmony and inner peace. An increased sense of love for all people. Clarity that helps you recognize situations that no longer serve you (relationships, career paths). Motivation and energy to follow your own unique path.

Can Kundalini energy awaken on its own?

Yes. Kundalini awakening is a natural human process. Many things can foster Kundalini awakening, for example, complete devotion to a deity, sex, or traumatic events. It can also happen spontaneously.

What happens during the session?

We will begin with an introduction to the practice, then proceed to meditation and 1-hour energy transmission. During the session, you lie on a yoga mat with your eyes closed. The facilitator will hold a high frequency to which your own Kundalini energy will begin to attune. There’s nothing for you to do but to FEEL and experience your energy. The facilitator may use physical touch to specific energy points or chakras. We will close with a community circle and some aftercare recommendations.

How many sessions do I need, and how often?

Each person’s process during Kundalini activation is unique. The experience varies from person to person and from session to session. In each session more energy is activated. The number and frequency of sessions depend entirely on your own preferences and needs.

Are there any contraindications for Kundalini activation?

  • KA is not recommended and is not safe for those prone to mania, delusion, or psychosis.
  • KA is not recommended and is not safe for people with epilepsy.
  • KA is not recommended for pregnant and post-natal women to avoid any risks due to the possible intensity of the practice.
  • If you are post-surgical, you affirm that you have your physician's approval to participate.
  • Age under 18 years old
Please inform the facilitator of any medical conditions or physical limitations before class.
